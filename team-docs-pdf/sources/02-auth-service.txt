AUTH SERVICE - THIET KE CHI TIET

1. Vai tro
Quan ly dang ky, dang nhap, quen mat khau, xac minh email va OTP.

2. Luong chinh
- Sign up: chuan hoa email -> tao user auth -> upsert profile.
- Sign in: xac thuc thong tin -> tra token + payload user.
- Forgot password: gui email reset voi redirect URL.
- Verify link/OTP: xac minh token va kich hoat luong tiep theo.

3. Ly do thiet ke
- Chuan hoa email tranh trung lap do khac hoa-thuong / khoang trang.
- upsert profile giu dong bo auth user va profile app.
- Tach controller/repository de de test va bao tri.

4. Luu y
- Redirect URL phai cau hinh dung theo moi truong.
- Kiem tra trung email truoc sign-up co the race trong tai cao, chap nhan duoc voi pham vi BTL.
